Top Destinations

Chembra Peak

Trek to the highest mountain in Wayanad with breathtaking views of tea and spice gardens. Elevation: 2,100m

📍 18 km from Kalpetta

Pookot Lake

The only natural freshwater lake in Wayanad, perfect for boating and fishing. Surrounded by lush greenery.

📍 12 km from Kalpetta

Banasura Dam

Asia's second-largest earth dam offers stunning views and water sports activities. Perfect for photography.

📍 25 km from Kalpetta

Soochipara Falls

A 200-meter cascade waterfall surrounded by dense forests. Perfect for swimming and adventure activities.

📍 20 km from Kalpetta

Wildlife Sanctuary

Home to leopards, tigers, and various species of birds. Jungle safari and nature walks available.

📍 30 km from Kalpetta

Jatayu Rock

A monolithic natural rock formation with a height of 350 meters. Offers panoramic views of the valley.

📍 28 km from Kalpetta

Must-See Attractions

Spy Garden Museum

Unique museum showcasing espionage equipment and artifacts. An underground heritage site with historical significance.

Spice Plantations

Tour the famous spice gardens growing cardamom, pepper, cinnamon, and other exotic spices unique to Wayanad.

Tea Gardens

Wander through rolling hills of green tea gardens. Experience tea picking and traditional tea processing.

Ancient Temples

Visit the historic Idukkiya Meenakshi Temple and Ambalavayal Ancient Palace. Learn about local history and culture.

Photography Points

Capture stunning landscapes at sunrise and sunset. Ideal locations for nature photography and videography.

Adventure Sports

Paragliding, rock climbing, camping, and trekking. Experienced guides available for all adventure activities.
